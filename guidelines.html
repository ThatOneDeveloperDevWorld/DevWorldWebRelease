🚨 Dev World Community Guidelines 🚨

Created: August 4th 2026 | Last Updated: November 2nd 2026
⚠️ 1. No drama
⚠️ 2. No spamming
⚠️ 3. No malicious links
• Phishing links, fake login links, info stealer links, cookie logger links, IP logger links, etc.
⚠️ 4. No doxxing
⚠️ 5. No harassment
• Includes bullying and targeted intimidation.
⚠️ 6. No inappropriate content
⚠️ 7. No self-promotion
⚠️ 8. No alts meant for bypassing bans
• Creating alternate accounts to evade an active ban will result in the immediate and permanent removal of all associated accounts.
⚠️ 9. Moderators and Admins have the final say.

🚨 Direct Ban System

Unlike the Discord server, Dev World does NOT use a warning count. Violations of these rules lead directly to account mutes or permanent bans from the platform.
