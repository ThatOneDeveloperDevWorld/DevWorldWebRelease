Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Welcome to Dev World. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data when you use our application and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or log in, we collect basic profile details via Firebase Authentication, such as your email address, display name, and unique user identifier to manage your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to:

  • Maintain and secure your user account.
  • Load your game data, profile info, and custom projects.
  • Enforce community guidelines and maintain platform safety.

3. Data Security

We use Google Firebase backend infrastructure and security controls to safeguard your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize Firebase Auth and Firestore for managing player accounts and core features. Google's privacy policies govern these backend features.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this policy, you can reach out via our official developer support channels.
